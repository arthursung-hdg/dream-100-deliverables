Is This For You?

Who this training is for (and who it isn't)

This is for you if…

  • ✓You've built a pile of company stock and a sizeable 401(k), and no one has ever shown you the tax bill waiting when you start pulling it out.
  • ✓You lie awake running the math, and you still can't say for certain how much you can spend each year without one day running out.
  • ✓You could probably retire now, but the market feels high, so you keep working "one more year" out of a fear no one has helped you size.
  • ✓Your advisor manages the money and your CPA files in April, and as far as you can tell the two have never once spoken.
  • ✓You're a professional, executive, or business owner within about ten years of retirement, with $1 million or more saved.

This is NOT for you if…

  • ✕You're decades from retirement and just getting started. This training is built for people near the finish line.
  • ✕You know your plan needs a second look, but you're not willing to set aside 45 minutes to actually do something about it.
  • ✕You're hunting for a hot stock tip or a way to beat the market, not a plan for keeping and spending what you already have.
  • ✕You want someone to fix one narrow thing, not look at how your investments, taxes, and retirement actually fit together.
