Is This For You?

Who this training is for (and who it isn't)

This is for you if…

  • ✓You're close to retirement with most of your savings still in the market, and a 2008-style drop right now would change everything.
  • ✓You lie awake wondering whether the balance will really last 30 years once the paychecks stop.
  • ✓You can afford the trip or the help for the grandkids, but you can't bring yourself to spend it without a guaranteed number.
  • ✓Your advisor's whole answer to a downturn is "ride it out," and that no longer feels good enough this close to retirement.
  • ✓You're within about ten years of retirement (or newly retired) with $500,000 or more saved.

This is NOT for you if…

  • ✕You're decades from retirement and focused on aggressive growth, not protecting what you have.
  • ✕You know your plan needs a second look, but you won't set aside 45 minutes to actually do something about it.
  • ✕You're after a hot stock tip or a way to beat the market, not a plan to protect and keep what you've built.
  • ✕You want a single product sold to you, not a coordinated look at income, taxes, protection, and legacy together.
