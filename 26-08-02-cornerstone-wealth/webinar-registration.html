Is This For You?

Who this training is for (and who it isn't)

This is for you if…

  • ✓You have a good CPA, and it has slowly dawned on you that everything he tells you is about a year that already ended. Nobody has ever called you in September.
  • ✓Every January "update the estate documents" goes on the list, and every December it is still on the list. The documents in that box were written for a family that has since changed.
  • ✓You own eleven different funds and have always called that diversified, until somebody pointed out that a large share of your net worth is still one company's stock.
  • ✓You have a CPA, someone for the investments, and an attorney who wrote the trust years ago. Each one is good at their piece. None of them has ever been in the same conversation.
  • ✓You are roughly 50 to 70, in or near Charlotte, Lake Norman, Huntersville, Greenville, Columbia or Aiken, with meaningful assets spread across old 401(k)s, brokerage accounts, IRAs, and perhaps a business or a rental.

This is NOT for you if…

  • ✕You are early in your career and still building the first account. This session assumes a financial life that already has several moving parts in it.
  • ✕You are curious, but you have no intention of doing anything with what you find. The training is only useful to somebody willing to act on what they see.
  • ✕You are looking for a hot tip, a product, or a shortcut. There is no recommendation, no allocation and nothing to buy anywhere in this session.
  • ✕You want one narrow fix and would rather not look at the tax return, the documents, the portfolio and the fees together. Looking at all four at once is the entire point.
