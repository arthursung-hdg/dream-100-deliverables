Is This For You?

Who this training is for (and who it isn't)

This is for you if…

  • ✓You spent 30 years becoming great at saving, and now that the paychecks are about to stop, you honestly have no idea how to turn the pile into monthly income.
  • ✓Every time the market has a bad week, you quietly push your retirement date back another year, and "one more year" is starting to feel like never.
  • ✓All you want is one honest answer to how much you can safely spend for the rest of your life, and every calculator hands you a different number.
  • ✓You've heard three "right answers" from three professionals (annuity, stay invested, bucket it), and not one of them asked what actually fits you.
  • ✓You're roughly 55 to 66, have saved a meaningful nest egg, and are within a few years of retirement, or you just crossed into it.

This is NOT for you if…

  • ✕You're decades from retirement and still in pure saving-and-accumulating mode.
  • ✕You know you should have an income plan, but you're not actually willing to sit down and build one this year.
  • ✕You're hunting for a hot stock tip, a "guaranteed" return, or a shortcut around the planning work.
  • ✕You want to be sold one product and skip looking at the whole picture: taxes, income, market timing, and longevity together.
